# DOC-2: FIN-01 refund approval policy

- Refund requests of $500.00 or less follow the standard path.
- Refund requests strictly above $500.00 require approval by an authorized finance approver.
- A finance approver may approve or reject a pending request.

## Not covered by this decision

The remaining refundable balance of an order, payment-provider behaviour, exchange rates and real-world compliance requirements. These are open questions, not permission to assume production safety.
